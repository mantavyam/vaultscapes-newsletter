Cursor open-sources MoE training kernel that runs 2.37x faster than top baselines
4,366 Likes

Cursor just open-sourced Mixture-of-Kittens (MoK), a tool that makes training large AI models much faster. Here is what you need to know.

AI models like Cursor's own coding assistant use a design called Mixture-of-Experts, where only a small group of "expert" sub-networks activates for each input. That MoE layer had become the biggest bottleneck, sometimes eating more than half of total training time.

MoK fixes this by doing something clever: it fuses all computation and communication into a single kernel, overlapping GPU math and inter-GPU networking, and fully eliminates CPU-GPU synchronization.

The results in production:

  • Runs up to 2.37x faster than public baselines
  • Raised end-to-end training throughput by 1.41x over their previous stack
  • Supports BF16 and MXFP8, covering both forward and backward passes

You can try MoK and explore the code on GitHub. It is Apache-2.0 licensed, so you can use it freely in your own projects.

Nous Research ships Hermes Agent v0.20.0 in new Herald release
3,886 Likes

Hermes Agent just dropped v0.20.0, and it's the biggest release in the project's history. Since v0.19.0: roughly 3,650 commits, 1,400 merged PRs, and 1,200 issues closed from over 650 contributors on a repo now sitting at 224,000 GitHub stars.

So what actually changed? Four big things:

  • Real-time voice , voice mode used to mean speak, wait, then listen to one long audio file. Now Hermes speaks clause-by-clause as the response streams, and you can interrupt it mid-sentence just by talking.
  • Agent-to-Agent protocol (A2A v1.0) , your Hermes instance can now talk directly to other AI agents using a standard protocol, meaning you can build multi-agent pipelines without custom glue code.
  • Outbound webhooks , Hermes can now push signed events to your external systems, so you can trigger dashboards, alerts, or automations automatically.
  • Grounded research , responses now come with verifiable citations, so you can actually trust what it tells you.

To grab the update, just run hermes update. That's it.

Liquid AI ships a 2.6B open-weight agentic model that runs fully on-device
3,683 Likes

Most small AI models fall apart the moment you ask them to plan across steps or call tools reliably. LFM2.5-2.6B from Liquid AI is a direct push against that wall.

It is a tiny but capable agent model that runs fully on your device, no cloud needed. Unlike cloud-based agents, you get free inference, real privacy, and the ability to run agents in parallel on local hardware at no extra cost per run.

The numbers are solid for its size:

  • 220 tokens/s on Apple M5 Max, 113 tok/s on AMD Ryzen, and around 30 tok/s on phones
  • Fits in under 2.5 GB of memory
  • Beats Qwen3.5-9B on tool use benchmarks despite being 4x smaller
  • Coding is the one area where larger models keep a clear lead

It ships with day-one support for llama.cpp, MLX, vLLM, SGLang, and ONNX. To use it, serve it behind an OpenAI-compatible endpoint and point your agent at it. Both base and post-trained versions are on Hugging Face now.
