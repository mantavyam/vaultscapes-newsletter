OpenAI reveals its first custom AI chip built with Broadcom
19,258 Likes

OpenAI just built its own chip. Meet Jalapeño, a custom processor built with Broadcom, designed from scratch specifically to run AI models faster and cheaper.

Here's the key thing to understand: this chip handles inference, meaning the part where a model actually answers your question. Not training. Just serving responses to users at massive scale.

Why does this matter? OpenAI was burning through Nvidia GPUs to power ChatGPT and Codex. Those are expensive and in short supply. A purpose-built chip means lower costs, faster responses, and more capacity.

  • Built in just 9 months, which is reportedly the fastest ever for a chip this complex
  • Early tests show better performance-per-watt than current top chips
  • Already running GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark in the lab
  • Designed to support any LLM, not just OpenAI's own models

OpenAI even used its own AI models to help design the chip. The takeaway: cheaper inference means cheaper API calls, faster Codex responses, and more reliable access when demand spikes.

OpenAI upgrades GPT-5.5 Instant with smarter intent and better recommendations
7,604 Likes

OpenAI just pushed an update to GPT-5.5 Instant, the model that powers most ChatGPT conversations. This is not a new model, it is a tune-up to the one already running by default.

So what actually changed? The model is now better at figuring out what you actually want, not just what you literally typed. It identifies the underlying goal behind a question, carries context across multiple turns, and follows complex instructions more reliably when a request includes several constraints.

The other big fix: it stops being stubborn. When you add constraints, clarify what you mean, or push back on an answer, the model adapts instead of just repeating its original response.

Here is what improved:

  • Intent understanding: reads the goal, not just the words
  • Multi-constraint handling: addresses all requirements in one shot
  • Shopping and local queries: better use of location context, combining product info, business details, and images more coherently

In the API, access it as "chat-latest". Paid users get it now, free users tomorrow.

Mistral releases OCR 4 with 72% win rate across 600+ real-world documents
6,187 Likes

Most OCR tools just dump raw text at you. Mistral OCR 4 does something smarter: it returns a full map of your document.

Every block of content gets a bounding box (its exact position on the page), a type label like title, table, equation, or signature, plus a confidence score so you know how sure the model is. That means you stop guessing where things are and start acting on them.

What does this unlock for you?

  • Cleaner RAG pipelines: classified blocks become better retrieval chunks, not messy text walls
  • Smarter agents: flag low-confidence regions for human review automatically
  • Compliance-safe: self-host the whole thing in a single container, documents never leave your environment
  • Broad language support: 170 languages, with real gains on rare ones where other tools fall apart

Pricing is $4 per 1,000 pages via API, or $2 with batch. Available now on Mistral Studio, Amazon SageMaker, and Microsoft Foundry.
