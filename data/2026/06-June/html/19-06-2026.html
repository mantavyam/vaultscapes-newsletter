Midjourney launches a new medical imaging division
26,349 Likes

Midjourney, the company you know for turning text into images, just announced Midjourney Medical , a full-body scanner. Nobody saw this coming.

Here is what it actually is. You step into a pool of water. Sound waves pass through your body from every angle. 60 seconds later, you have a 3D map of your entire body. No radiation, no magnets, just ultrasound.

The specs are wild:

  • 500,000 transducers (tiny sensors) firing sound waves through you simultaneously
  • 17 gigabytes of data processed every second
  • A few dollars per scan, not hundreds
  • First location opens in a San Francisco spa in 2027

The goal is catching health problems early, before symptoms show up. Think of it like version control for your body , scan regularly, spot changes over time.

They are starting with body composition data (fat, muscle, bone density) while working toward FDA approval for full diagnostics. Midjourney has never shipped a physical product before, so the 2027 timeline is the real test.

OpenCut is a free open-source video editor with 55K GitHub stars and no watermarks
23,605 Likes

CapCut locks basic features behind a paywall, slaps watermarks on your exports, and uploads your footage to their servers. A group of devs got fed up and built OpenCut, a fully open-source alternative that just hit 55,000 GitHub stars.

Here's what you actually get:

  • Multi-track timeline editor with real-time preview, no watermarks, no subscriptions
  • Runs in your browser, on desktop, and on mobile, all from one codebase
  • Your files never leave your device, everything is stored locally
  • MCP Server built in, meaning you can connect AI agents to automate editing tasks
  • MIT license, so you can fork it, modify it, ship it in your own product

The current version works today. A full rewrite in Rust (a faster, lower-level language) is in progress, adding a plugin system and scripting support.

To try it: clone the repo, run 

bun install
, then 
bun run dev
. Done.

Anthropic launches Claude Design to build UIs using your real design system
13,223 Likes

Claude Design just got a big update, and the core idea is simple: stop letting AI invent your brand from scratch every time.

The old problem? You'd prompt Claude Design to build a UI, and it would make up its own buttons, colors, and spacing. Every project looked slightly different. Now you can import your actual design system from a GitHub repo, a design file, or a raw upload. Claude builds with your real components, then checks its own output against your system before you even see it.

Here's what's new and worth knowing:

  • Import your design system once, stay on brand across every project
  • Edit directly on the canvas by dragging, resizing, and aligning elements
  • Use /design-sync in Claude Code to pull your design system into your terminal workflow
  • Export to PDF, PowerPoint, Canva, Vercel, and more
  • Shared usage limits with Claude Code, so less quota burn

Find it at claude.ai/design on any paid plan.
