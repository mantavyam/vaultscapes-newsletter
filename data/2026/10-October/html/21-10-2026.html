DeepSeek open-sources its production inference stack to cut AI compute costs
32,366 Likes

DeepSeek just open-sourced DeepInfer, the actual engine running their models in production. Not a demo. Not a stripped-down version. The real thing.

Think of an inference framework as the software layer between your model and your users. It decides how fast responses come back, how many users you can serve at once, and how much that costs you. DeepSeek is handing that over for free.

Here's what's inside:

  • Speculative decoding via DSpark: drafts 5 tokens ahead, checks only the most confident ones, cutting verification work by ~30% and boosting throughput 4-7%
  • Distributed inference: splits work across many GPUs instead of choking on one
  • Structured outputs: constrain responses to JSON schemas or regex patterns out of the box
  • deepseek-recipe: token encoding that matches the official API exactly, so your self-hosted version behaves identically

Hardware support: NVIDIA SM100 and Huawei Ascend 950. Older Nvidia architectures are not supported yet. License is Apache 2.0, so you can use it commercially. Future DeepSeek model updates land here too.

Anthropic uses Claude to design 300,000 proteins targeting deadly scorpion toxins
7,389 Likes

Scorpions sting 1.2 million people every year. Antivenoms are expensive and often out of stock. Treating a sting can mean finding a molecule that physically grabs onto the toxin before it reaches your nerve cells. That search normally takes years.

Anthropic just showed Claude can do a big chunk of that work autonomously.

Claude chose target sites on five scorpion toxins, set its own scoring thresholds, generated 318,484 candidate proteins using 12 design tools, and picked 180 to send to two labs for testing. All from a single prompt and four follow-ups.

Here is what the lab results showed:

  • 211 designs were tested, and 17 met strict binding criteria.
  • The strongest hits bind the Mexican Cn2 toxin at single-digit nanomolar affinity.
  • No design bound more than one toxin, and none bound the Brazilian Ts1.
  • All 211 designs and lab data are publicly released.

The 17 candidates have not yet been shown to neutralize venom in cells or protect animals from stings. But this shows AI can now run early drug discovery searches that used to require entire lab teams.

Anthropic drops 400+ free ready-to-use agents and skills for Claude
5,197 Likes

Anthropic just dropped a massive free library of 400+ agents, skills, and plugins for Claude. Think of skills as pre-built instruction sets that tell Claude exactly how to handle specific tasks, no setup required.

Here's what's available across the main repos:

  • knowledge-work-plugins: 11 role-based plugins covering sales, legal, finance, data, marketing, product, and more. Each one connects Claude to your real tools like Slack, Jira, HubSpot, and Snowflake.
  • claude-plugins-official: 202 agents, 184 skills, and 105 commands across 94 plugins built for Claude Code. Covers code review, debugging, security, and CI/CD.
  • claude-quickstarts: 25 skills covering the full dev cycle, from writing specs to browser testing to security review. Best starting point.

Every plugin is just markdown and JSON files. No code, no build steps. You pick only what you need, customize it for your stack, and Claude starts working like it knows your team.

Install with: claude plugin marketplace add anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins, then install individual plugins per role.
