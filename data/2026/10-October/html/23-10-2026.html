Anthropic launches Claude Security to scan and track code vulnerabilities automatically
11,456 Likes

Old security scanners had a big problem: they gave you a snapshot. Code kept moving, but your findings stayed frozen in time. Claude Security just fixed that.

Connect your repos once from GitHub, GitLab, or any Git provider. Claude does a deep first scan, then keeps watching. Every time code changes, it re-scans automatically. No manual triggers needed.

The really useful part: fix tracking. When your team ships a patch, Claude checks the changed code and marks the issue resolved, even if the fix happened outside Claude Security. And if the bug sneaks back in later, the finding reopens on its own.

Each project also gets a threat model (a written summary of where your code is most at risk) that Claude generates from your actual code, and your team can edit it.

  • Findings delivered via a new API and MCP connector, so they plug into your existing tools
  • Spans multiple repos, catching bugs that cross service boundaries
  • $25K/month in free credits for two months for eligible orgs

It is live now in public beta for Claude Enterprise customers.

Nous Research's Hermes agent builds and trains a robot duck sim in one prompt
9,791 Likes

Jensen Huang just closed his GTC Berlin keynote with a live Hermes Agent demo, and it was a proper flex for open source AI.

Here is what happened. Jensen typed one prompt. Hermes installed itself, configured itself, and got to work. No hand-holding. That single prompt kicked off a full robot training pipeline.

Hermes then coordinated a chain of tools to train Hugging Face's tiny Microduck robot:

  • Nemotron planned the obstacle course
  • GPT-6 Astra built it using FreeCAD, Blender, Omniverse, Newton, and Isaac Lab
  • Thousands of simulated ducks trained in parallel until the policy was ready
  • That policy then ran on the real physical robot

Hermes is MIT-licensed, runs locally or in the cloud, and supports 300+ models with no vendor lock-in. You can chain local models, cloud models, and real-world tools yourself, without waiting for any company to build that workflow for you.

That is the whole point. You own the stack.

Grok Bot now controls your phone's calendar, reminders, and contacts
8,528 Likes

Grok Bot just got a lot more useful on your phone. It can now tap into your native phone apps, not just chat with you.

Six apps are now supported:

  • Messages - read and send texts
  • Calendar - check your schedule and add events
  • Reminders - set and check reminders
  • Contacts - find and add people
  • Location - find places near you
  • Home - control lights and locks

You grant access app by app. Want it to manage reminders but not messages? Done. You stay in control of your data.

The big deal here: your phone already syncs calendars and contacts from iCloud, Google, and Exchange. Grok sees all of that in one shot, with no extra setup needed per account.

Just update the app on iOS or Android and you are good to go.
