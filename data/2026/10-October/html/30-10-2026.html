Anthropic ships Claude mobile update with multi-account, CarPlay, and 33 languages
13,185 Likes

Claude's mobile apps just got a solid round of updates. Here's what changed and why it matters.

  • Multi-account switching on iOS and Android: stay logged into multiple accounts and flip between them without signing out.
  • iPad split view: whatever Claude creates now opens beside the chat, not on top of it. Tap to go full screen when you need a closer look.
  • 33 languages total: Bengali, Swahili, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian, Vietnamese, and more are now supported across all platforms.
  • CarPlay voice mode on iPhone: ask Claude questions while driving, hands-free.
  • Faster, smarter voice: responds quicker, handles noisy environments better, and now runs on more Android phones.
  • Chromebook and foldable support: resizable sidebar, trackpad, right-click, and keyboard shortcuts, just like the desktop app.

Coming soon: pull-down search on iPhone and iPad to find past chats, projects, or settings instantly.

OpenAI gives Pro and Business users up to $500 monthly in free API credits
10,332 Likes

OpenAI just made your ChatGPT subscription pull double duty. Pro and Business subscribers can now claim free API credits every month, meaning you can build real apps without paying extra.

Think of the API like a direct line to OpenAI's models that you control from your own code. These credits let you call that line for free each month.

Here is how the credits break down:

  • Pro $100/$200/$500 plans each get matching credits (pay $200, get $200 in API credits)
  • Business Standard seats get $20 each, Premium seats get $100 each, pooled up to $500 per workspace
  • Credits expire at end of each billing cycle and do not roll over

To claim: go to ChatGPT billing settings, find the API credits benefit, and link it to an API organization you own. You also need Owner permissions on that API org.

You can use credits on agents, images, and live API features. Small teams get real value here.

NVIDIA releases open-weight speech model that tops ASR leaderboards with 4B active parameters
9,693 Likes

NVIDIA just dropped Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, and it just took the top spot among open-weight speech models. Here's why it matters.

Most transcription setups make you stitch together separate tools: one for transcription, one for translation, one to figure out who's speaking. Nemotron collapses all of that into a single model you can download and run yourself.

  • Transcribes audio in 40 languages, handles recordings up to 1 hour long
  • Tells apart up to 8 speakers in a conversation automatically
  • Translates 31 languages to or from English
  • You can prompt it: "redact PII" or "add speaker labels" in plain text
  • Ranked #1 open-weight model on Hugging Face Open ASR Leaderboard at 5.33% word error rate

The efficiency angle is real: it runs on roughly 4B active parameters despite being a 30B model, matching accuracy of a 24B competitor at one-sixth the compute cost.

It's free, open-weight, and commercially usable. Grab it on Hugging Face.
