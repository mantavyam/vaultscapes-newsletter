DeepSeek open-sources its production inference engine to cut compute costs
49,039 Likes

DeepSeek just open-sourced DeepInfer, the actual engine running their models in production. Not a demo. Not a research paper. The real thing.

Think of an inference engine as the software layer that takes a trained AI model and makes it answer questions fast, at scale, without melting your server budget. That is what DeepInfer does.

Here is what is inside:

  • Speculative decoding via DSpark: a draft model guesses tokens ahead, a confidence filter trims ~30% of checks, netting a 4-7% throughput gain
  • Distributed inference to split work across multiple chips
  • Structured outputs so responses follow a set format
  • Runs on Nvidia SM100 and Huawei Ascend 950 chips

They also dropped deepseek-recipe, a token encoding library that mirrors the official DeepSeek API exactly. Self-host it and your outputs behave identically to the hosted version. No more subtle mismatches.

Both repos are on GitHub under Apache 2.0. Future model code lands here too.

Anthropic previews Claude Duet, a proactive agent that works while you're away
42,516 Likes

Most AI tools sit there waiting for you to type something. Claude Duet flips that. It comes to you.

Anthropic just launched a research preview of Duet, a standalone app where Claude acts as a proactive agent. Think of it less like a chatbot and more like a background worker that monitors your apps, surfaces what matters, and handles tasks while you are away.

Here is what it actually does:

  • Sends you a morning brief from your connected apps
  • Monitors email, calendars, and Slack, then pings you only when something needs you
  • Connects to 6,000+ tools including invoicing and scheduling
  • Keeps running in the cloud even when your device is off
  • Asks your approval before sending messages, payments, or deleting anything

It runs on Claude Opus 5.5 and pulls in your existing Claude memory and connected apps, so setup is minimal. It never sees your passwords, and a dedicated safety layer guards against leaking sensitive info.

Access is via waitlist for Pro and Max subscribers in the US, at no extra cost, though background tasks count toward your usage limits.

Unitree unveils a kung fu humanoid robot with 8-hour battery life
20,309 Likes

Unitree just dropped the H2-X Kung Fu Legend Guardian, a humanoid robot built for security and rescue. Think less warehouse helper, more bodyguard with a black belt.

Here is what it can actually do:

  • Run for 8 hours on a single charge, nearly triple the base H2's 3-hour battery life
  • Jump off 4-meter platforms and land without falling over
  • Lift cars to free people trapped underneath
  • Execute real martial arts moves using an AI model that predicts opponent actions in real-time, no human controlling it
  • Pack 2,070 TOPS of onboard AI compute, roughly the processing muscle needed to run complex decisions locally

It stands 182cm tall, weighs 70kg, and has 31 movable joints. Unitree actually warns you to stay 2-3 meters away while it operates. That tells you everything about how much force those joints generate.

No pricing or release date announced yet. The base H2 starts at $29,900 for context.
