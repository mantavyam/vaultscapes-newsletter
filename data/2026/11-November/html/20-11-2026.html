Nous Research brings its Hermes agent to Microsoft Teams and Azure
5,453 Likes

Nous Research just dropped Hermes Enterprise, and it lives right inside the tools your company already uses.

Here is the setup. Most AI agents force you to leave your workflow to use them. Hermes flips that. You stay in Teams, hand it a task, and get the result back in the same chat thread.

What you can actually do with it:

  • Assign financial analysis, document drafts, or inbox triage directly inside Teams
  • Download Hermes Desktop from the Microsoft Store for Windows
  • Run agent tasks in isolated Azure Container Apps sandboxes, so nothing bleeds between jobs
  • Deploy the whole thing inside your own Azure tenant if your company has strict data rules

The most interesting part is Collective Wisdom. When someone on your team figures out a great way to handle a recurring task, Hermes saves it as a skill. After a quick review, the whole team can use it. Your org's best practices build up over time automatically.

It is MIT licensed and open-source, so you can inspect exactly what it does.

Google releases Gemini 4 Neon with 1M context at $3 per million tokens
5,017 Likes

Google just dropped Gemini 4 Neon, and it sits right between their mid-tier and top-tier models. Think of it as the sweet spot: near-flagship smarts, but faster and cheaper to run.

Here is what it can do:

  • #1 on the Coding Agent Index (58.6), beating Claude Opus 5.5 on CursorBench 65.4% vs 58.1%
  • Best-in-class science reasoning: 84% on RiemannBench, 71.3% on HLE-Diamond
  • 1 million token context window, so you can feed it entire codebases, PDFs, spreadsheets, audio, and video all at once
  • Four thinking levels (low, medium, high, xhigh), letting you dial cost vs. intelligence per task
  • 32% cheaper per task than Gemini 4 Carbon at the default setting

Pricing is $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output. You can hit it right now via the Gemini API or AI Studio using model ID gemini-4-neon.

OpenAI ships GPT-Live-1.1 to API with standby mode cutting idle costs 60%
4,186 Likes

GPT-Live-1.1 just landed in the API. This is the same voice model that has been powering ChatGPT Plus and Pro for weeks. Now you can plug it into your own apps.

So what does it actually do better? It handles accents more naturally across languages, understands precise details like letters and numbers, and follows instructions more reliably. Think voice agents that don't fumble when a user spells out an email address.

The big new addition is Standby mode. Voice calls cost money even when nobody is talking. Standby solves that. Set a silence timeout between 5 and 60 seconds. When the user goes quiet, the model pauses but the call stays open at $0.02 per minute instead of the usual $0.05. That's a 60% drop in idle costs.

  • Model ID: gpt-live-1.1, endpoint: v1/live/sessions
  • Standby works on both GPT-Live-1 and GPT-Live-1.1
  • Supports streaming and function calling, not fine-tuning or image inputs

Already on GPT-Live-1? Swap the model ID. That's it.
