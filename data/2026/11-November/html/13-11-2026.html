Anthropic launches Claude Meetings to join video calls and build docs live
56,477 Likes

Your team is mid-call, someone says "let's write this up" and... nothing happens until after. Claude Meetings fixes that gap.

Anthropic just dropped a beta that lets Claude join your Google Meet call as a visible participant. It listens, and starts working while you are still talking.

Here is what it actually does during the call:

  • Answers questions you ask out loud or in chat
  • Builds docs and whiteboards in real time as the conversation unfolds
  • Takes notes and logs action items automatically
  • Pulls data from connected work apps when you give it permission

When the call ends, everything Claude built, notes, transcript, docs, lands on a dedicated page in claude.ai. No chasing people for summaries.

On privacy: Claude saves a transcript but not audio, and by default it leaves if outside guests join the call.

It is live in beta for Team and Enterprise plans on Google Meet, with more platforms coming later.

Anthropic opens private beta for self-hosted Claude agents on your own cloud
10,448 Likes

Anthropic just dropped self-hosted Claude Managed Agents into private beta, and it's a big deal for teams that can't send sensitive data to outside servers.

Here's the simple version: Claude Managed Agents is an AI that runs tasks on its own, like an autopilot for your codebase. Until now, Anthropic ran all the infrastructure behind it. Now you can run it yourself, on your own servers or cloud account.

What stays in your environment:

  • The agent server, database, and sandboxes (isolated workspaces where code runs safely)
  • Your credentials and session history, so nothing leaks out
  • Your choice of model provider: Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, Google, or your own internal gateway

This opens up tasks that were previously off-limits, like fixing broken builds, triaging alerts, and managing bug backlogs, all with access to your internal systems.

It's private beta only right now, limited to eligible orgs. To get in, reach out to your Anthropic account team.
