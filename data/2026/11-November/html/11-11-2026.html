Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 solves open physics problems and designs working hardware
58,460 Likes

Anthropic just dropped Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2, and the demo tasks are genuinely hard to believe.

From a single paragraph prompt, Fable 5.2 designed three working electronic devices. It wrote specs, picked components, routed circuit boards, and programmed the software. Each board was ready to manufacture in under a day, with zero modifications needed.

That is not all it pulled off during testing:

  • Mapped full chemistry routes for 4 complex molecules in ~30 minutes, rated at early grad-student level by outside experts
  • Beat 72 out of 203 open math optimization problems (think airline crew scheduling) and proved 45 solutions were the best possible
  • Solved two open physics problems about black holes, one unsolved for 23 years

Speed-wise, outputs are 30% faster than 5.1, at a lower price. Fable 5.2 is live now on all paid plans. Mythos 5.2, the less restricted version for cybersecurity and biomedical work, requires verified org access.

Anthropic ships Claude Whiteboards, Sheets, and Data tools for teams
55,109 Likes

Claude just became a full workspace. Anthropic shipped four new tools at once, and they all live inside your Claude chat.

  • Claude Whiteboards: describe a messy plan, Claude builds a shared visual board your team can edit together. It can also turn the board into a doc or slide deck when you're done.
  • Claude Sheets: ask for a tracker or budget, get a working spreadsheet with tabs, formulas, and 144 functions. Exports to Excel. Google Workspace plugin included. Up to 100,000 cells per sheet.
  • Claude Data: connects to Snowflake, Salesforce, and similar tools. Ask a question, get back a dashboard, a written summary, or a narrated video of your metrics.
  • Interactive Connectors: open Adobe, Atlassian, or HubSpot in full inside Claude. Make changes yourself or ask Claude to help.

Sheets and Whiteboards are in beta on paid plans. Claude Data is fully live now. The Claude Marketplace has over 9,000 connectors available.

OpenAI lets businesses build custom chat agents inside ChatGPT
14,588 Likes

OpenAI just turned ChatGPT into a storefront. Here's what that means.

Businesses in ChatGPT is now in beta. It lets companies claim a profile, manage their info, and deploy a custom AI agent, all inside ChatGPT, which now reaches 1.2 billion people weekly.

Here's what you can actually do with it:

  • Claim and verify your business through Business Hub, then update details and add live product feeds with pricing and availability.
  • Build a Business Agent, basically a chatbot trained on your own data, so users can compare your products and ask questions without leaving ChatGPT.
  • See aggregated analytics: mentions, website clicks, and common topics where your business gets cited.
  • Shopify merchants can connect directly from Business Hub or the Shopify admin panel.

Privacy note: user conversations are never shared with you. You only see aggregated trends, not individual chats.

Business Agents are currently US-only for ecommerce and online services. Business Hub is available globally.
