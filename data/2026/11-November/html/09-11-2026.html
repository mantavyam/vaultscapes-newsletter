Nous Research fine-tunes Qwen3 to cut pro-China propaganda rate from 22% to 1%
18,974 Likes

Chinese open models are currently the strongest you can run locally. But there's a catch: they ship with built-in political bias, meaning they dodge or spin answers on topics like democracy, free speech, and privacy to match the Chinese government's position.

Nous Research just fixed that. They took Alibaba's Qwen3.8-27B and retrained it to produce Arete-27B. Here's what changed:

  • Propaganda rate dropped from 22% to under 1%, matching Western models
  • Fact score improved from 2.32 to 2.98 out of 5
  • General performance stayed flat, 65.60% vs 65.77% on Hermes Bench
  • No reinforcement learning used, just plain supervised fine-tuning on 53 million tokens

The big takeaway: political bias sits shallow in these models. You can swap it out without breaking anything else. The weights, eval set, and full training data are all openly available right now on Hugging Face.

Open-source Chrome extension translates pages using a local LLM, no account needed
5,435 Likes

Sakutto is a free, open-source Chrome extension that translates web pages entirely on your device. No account, no server, no data leaving your browser.

The magic here: it runs a small AI model called PLaMo 3 Translate (a 0.8B model, meaning a compact AI small enough to live on your laptop) directly inside Chrome. Your text never touches an external server.

Here's what you can do with it:

  • Translate any full web page with Alt+A (or Option+A on Mac)
  • Translate selected text with Alt+S, or paste text manually in the popup
  • Switch between translated-only and bilingual view (original + translation side by side) with Alt+B
  • Supports 53 languages, with 11 more handled by Chrome's built-in translator as a fallback

It's MIT licensed, fully open source, and installable from the Chrome Web Store right now. Worth a star on GitHub.

OpenCode ships browser control so your coding agent uses your real tabs and logins
5,418 Likes

AI agents that control browsers are nothing new. But they always had one annoying problem: they'd spin up a blank browser with zero logins, zero cookies, zero context. You'd have to re-authenticate everything. Painful.

OpenCode Browse fixes that. Your agent now controls your actual browser, the one already signed into GitHub, Stripe, your staging environment, everything.

Here's what it can do for you:

  • Open and drive tabs automatically, taking screenshots and recording network traffic
  • Hand control back to you for logins, 2FA, or payments, then pick up where it left off
  • Works with Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Zed, and any MCP-compatible tool
  • Supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, and most Chromium browsers

To get started, install the Chrome extension, then run opencode browser install once. That's it. Everything stays local, nothing goes to the cloud.

The big unlock: testing flows that require real auth, like checkout pages or dashboards, without writing a single login script.
